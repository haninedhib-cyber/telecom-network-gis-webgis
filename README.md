## 🌐 Project Overview

**Connecting telecommunications infrastructure with geographic information.**

This repository presents my engineering internship experience at **Tunisie Telecom — Regional Directorate of Mahdia, Tunisia**, completed as part of my ICT engineering studies at the **National Engineering School of Tunis (ENIT)**.

The internship provided an opportunity to explore telecommunications access networks, geographic infrastructure representation, spatial databases, and WebGIS technologies.

It also involved setting up a local GIS/WebGIS environment to understand how spatial information can be stored, managed, published, and visualized through interactive maps.

## 🚀 Practical Work & Learning Outcomes

- **Network knowledge:** explored copper and fiber-optic access network architectures.
- **Geographic representation:** studied how telecommunications equipment and networks are represented in a GIS.
- **Spatial data management:** worked with a local PostgreSQL/PostGIS environment.
- **Geospatial publishing:** explored the publication of geographic layers through GeoServer.
- **Metadata management:** explored GeoNetwork for geospatial resource cataloguing.
- **Web mapping:** implemented a local WebGIS interface for viewing layers, inspecting attributes, managing geographic entities, and measuring distances.
