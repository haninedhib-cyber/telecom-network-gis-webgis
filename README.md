## 🎯 Internship Objectives

The main objectives of this internship were to:

- Discover the organization and activities of Tunisie Telecom.
- Understand the architecture of telecommunications access networks.
- Explore copper and fiber-optic network infrastructures.
- Understand the role of GIS in organizing and visualizing telecommunications network data.
- Explore the use of spatial databases and geospatial services.
- Develop a local GIS/WebGIS environment to study the management and visualization of spatial data.

---

## 🌐 Telecommunications Access Networks

During the internship, I explored different access network technologies and their role in connecting subscribers to telecommunications infrastructure.

Topics covered include:

- **Copper Networks:** Understanding the traditional copper access network.
- **FTTx:** Discovering fiber-optic access network architectures.
- **GPON (Gigabit Passive Optical Network):** Understanding the main components of a fiber-optic access network.
- **AirPON:** Exploring an approach to fiber deployment and its network architecture.

These observations helped me understand how telecommunications infrastructure connects the network operator to end users.

---

## 🗺️ Geographic Information Systems (GIS)

A major part of the internship focused on the role of GIS in telecommunications.

GIS makes it possible to organize, manage, analyze, and visualize geographically referenced data. In a telecommunications context, it can help represent network infrastructure and associate technical information with geographic locations.

The work explored the use of GIS technologies to manage spatial data and make network information easier to visualize and understand.

## 🏗️ Local GIS/WebGIS Environment

The project explored a local environment for managing and visualizing telecommunications-related spatial data.

The general workflow is:

1. **Data Storage:** Store spatial and descriptive information in PostgreSQL/PostGIS.
2. **Data Preparation:** Use QGIS to inspect and manage geographic layers.
3. **Data Publication:** Explore GeoServer for publishing geospatial data through web services.
4. **Metadata Management:** Explore GeoNetwork for organizing geospatial metadata.
5. **Web Visualization:** Explore web mapping concepts for displaying geographic information.

The objective is to understand how these technologies can work together to support the management and visualization of spatial data.

**Note:** This repository documents the internship report and the local technical work explored during the internship. It does not claim that the environment was deployed as a production system at Tunisie Telecom.
